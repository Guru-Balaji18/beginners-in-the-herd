# Hillsborough Regional STEM Fair and SSEF Florida: Research Plan Requirements (2026-2027)

Research date: 2026-11-20. Current 2026-2027 documents were found for both fairs: the HCPS 6-12 timeline, the HR STEM Fair Google Site, the 2026-27 SSEF Rules Supplement, and the ISEF 2027 Form 1A. Older (2025-26) documents are used only where noted.

## Q1. Hillsborough Regional STEM Fair (HRSEF/HR STEM Fair): template, required sections, page limits, platform, dates

### Takeaway
Hillsborough (grades 6-12) publishes no research-plan template or handbook of its own online. It tells students to use the ISEF forms (1, 1A with Research Plan, 1B, 2A, 3) plus the SSEF Florida forms, and it collects them through a Google Site with Google Forms, not zFairs or STEM Wizard. The research plan is due before experimentation, and the typed final plan is uploaded by **January 11, 2027**. Judging is **February 17, 2027** at the Tampa Convention Center.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The Jan 11 instruction to remove "all template formatting and notes" from the research plan suggests Hillsborough or the schools hand out a research-plan template with guidance notes, possibly a Word version of the ISEF Research Plan/Project Addendum headings. I could not find it publicly. It most likely reaches students through teachers or the adult-sponsor training.
- In practice the required headers are the ISEF 2026-27 Research Plan/Project Addendum headings (Q2) plus the SSEF supplement additions. Hillsborough points to both and adds no headers of its own that I could find.
- **Form 3 for all projects** looks stricter than ISEF, where Form 3 is normally needed only for hazardous work. Even a pure data-analysis project should expect to file Form 3 with the Direct Supervisor before starting.
- The abstract appears in the Jan 11 upload list even though the abstract is written after experimentation. So for a 6-12 project, data collection, analysis and abstract writing all have to be finished before the Jan 11 upload, which is about three weeks after the school-fair deadline.

### Gaps
- I found no public Hillsborough research-plan template, checklist or handbook for grades 6-12. The "Quality Research Plan" guidance is given at adult-sponsor training and was not online.
- No page limit or formatting rules (font, length) for the research plan were found for Hillsborough. The only formatting cue is "typed" with template notes removed.
- The Junior and Senior upload pages were not opened. The timeline links `.../junior-project-submissions` and `.../senior-project-submissions`, but the hub links to `.../junior-project-submissions-hr-stem-fair` and `.../senior-project-submissions-hrsef`. One older link (`.../paperwork-requirements`) returned 404, so some timeline links may be stale.
- Small inconsistency: the "before experimentation" list and the paperwork page include Form 2A, but the Jan 11 upload list on the timeline leaves it out. Treat 2A as required. — [Timeline](https://www.hillsboroughschools.org/page/stem-fair-grades-6-12-timeline/) vs [Paperwork page](https://sites.google.com/view/hillsborough-stem-fair/hrsef-paperwork-requirements)

## Q2. SSEF Florida (FFFS): research plan, abstract and forms; ISEF forms; Florida-specific rules

### Takeaway
SSEF requires the ISEF 2026-27 forms and rules in full, plus the stricter **2026-27 SSEF Rules Supplement**. For the research plan, the supplement adds these requirements:
- written before experimentation, replicable, and containing **no data or conclusions**
- materials with GHS/NFPA ratings
- step-by-step procedures
- a clear student-vs-adult role split
- the location of each phase
- an addendum for substantial changes
- **at least five bibliography sources**
- full citations (not bare URLs) for **open-access data repositories**
- AI use documented in the bibliography

Abstracts must be on the official **72nd SSEF Abstract Form**. The fair runs **March 30-April 1, 2027** at the RP Funding Center, Lakeland.

### Cited Findings
**Basics**
- The 72nd SSEF is March 30-April 1, 2027 at the RP Funding Center in Lakeland, run by the Florida Foundation for Future Scientists (FFFS). It is for grades 6-12: Junior 6-8, Senior 9-12. Each student enters one project covering at most 12 continuous months of research. Teams are 2-3 members. — [SSEF Welcome](https://ssefflorida.com/welcome/); [SSEF Home](https://ssefflorida.com/)
- Affiliated fairs "must operate under the ISEF Rules and Guidelines and the SSEF of Florida Supplemental Rules." Students must "use Intel ISEF forms from the start of research." Students without the proper approvals or forms "in the proper timeline may not qualify for competition at the SSEF." — [SSEF Welcome](https://ssefflorida.com/welcome/)
- "It will also be required that the OFFICIAL SSEF ABSTRACT be used for entry into the SSEF of Florida." — [SSEF Welcome](https://ssefflorida.com/welcome/)
- SSEF links the ISEF forms and the ISEF Rules 2026/27 book. "The forms should be filled out and signed before any research takes place. (Only Forms 2C, 5B, 7, and the abstract are done after the research.)" Dates use MM/DD/YY. Some SSEF rules "are more stringent than" ISEF "and take precedence." — [SSEF Rules & FAQs](https://ssefflorida.com/rules/)
- SSEF Documents & Forms is an embedded Google Drive with folders: Rules, For Students, For Teachers, 2026 Fall Workshop Resources, and others. — [SSEF Documents & Forms](https://ssefflorida.com/documents/)

**2026-27 SSEF Rules Supplement** (linked as "Revised SSEF Rules Supplement" from [SSEF Home](https://ssefflorida.com/); file title "2026-27 SSEF Rules Supplement FINAL"). All points below are from the [2026-27 SSEF Rules Supplement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ImzJHKi8CdMCD2b_YQWpJaAhyEbQcH74/view).

*Abstract*
- All abstracts for the 2027 SSEF "MUST be on the approved 72nd SSEF Abstract Form."
- Only the official stamped abstract may be displayed, vertically and separate from the board. No copies go to judges or the public.

*Research plan, in addition to ISEF p. 32*
- "detailed step-by-step description of the student's involvement in procedures … written prior to experimentation. It should be fully and clearly replicable by a reviewer. **The research plan must not include data, conclusions, or unapproved procedures.**"
- Projects sent without a complete research plan "will not be approved for competition … resubmissions will not be permitted."
- The plan must include:
  - (a) a materials list, with chemicals' concentrations, quantities and GHS/NFPA ratings, plus consumables, apparatus, organisms and participants
  - (b) procedures as specific steps, with safety, aseptic technique and disposal where applicable
  - (c) a clear distinction between student and adult steps (for example "Role of Student" and "Role of Mentor")
  - (d) where each phase is done and who supervises it
- A "substantial addition, deletion, or clarification" to the procedures requires an addendum that explains the changes. Some changes may need SRC/IRB re-approval.

*Bibliography*
- "If a student uses procedures taken from a published study, **data from open-access data repositories**, laboratory standards, or equipment/kit manual, a complete citation (ie, not simply a URL) **MUST** be included with the research plan."
- "The bibliography should include a **minimum of five sources** relevant to the project, not including rulebooks or SDS citations."
- "The use of Artificial Intelligence must be documented in the bibliography."

*Display*
- No electricity. Table-top only, at most 66 in high (from the table), 30 in deep and 48 in wide.

*SRC review*
- Every project entering SSEF is reviewed and must be approved by the SSEF SRC before competition. Rules questions go to flscifair.src@gmail.com.

**Changes from 2025-26**
- The 2025-26 supplement had the same research-plan rules (written in past tense) but no five-source minimum. It also included display rules: acknowledgements in one text-only section with no logos, and every photo, graph or table individually cited. — [2025-26 SSEF Rules Supplement (St. Johns copy)](https://www.stjohns.k12.fl.us/stem-fair/wp-content/uploads/sites/182/2025/07/2025-26-SSEF-Rules-Supplement.pdf)

**ISEF 2026-27 Research Plan/Project Addendum (Form 1A instructions)**
All points below are from [ISEF 2027 Form 1A + Research Plan instructions](https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/ISEF/2027/Forms/1A-Student-Checklist-Research-Plan-Instructions.pdf).
- A plan is "required for ALL projects. The plan must be written BEFORE experimentation." Changes made before competing go in an addendum, and some changes need SRC/IRB re-review.
- Headings: (a) RATIONALE; (b) RESEARCH QUESTION(S), HYPOTHESIS(ES), ENGINEERING GOAL(S), EXPECTED OUTCOMES; (c) List of materials, Procedures/Methodology (with data collection methods, "the source of data used," and student vs supervising-adult steps), Risk and Safety, Data Analysis; (d) ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS, which includes documenting AI use "with a prompt log or an explanation"; (e) BIBLIOGRAPHY. AI-generated citations are "discouraged."
- Form 1A item 10, "Source of Data," has a checkbox for "publicly available."
- A third-party summary of 2026-27 ISEF changes gives the same research plan items (Rationale; RQ/Hypothesis/Engineering Goal/Expected Outcomes; Materials; Procedures; Risk and Safety; Data Analysis; Bibliography), notes they now appear under Eligibility/Limitations, and calls Form 2A (Student Support Disclosure) a new form required for all projects. — [science-fair.org (SCVSEFA) 2027 changes](https://science-fair.org/2027-changes-from-previous-rules-procedures/)

**ISEF abstract and forms**
- ISEF abstract: "a (maximum) 250-word, one-page abstract which summarizes the current year's work," submitted after experimentation. Regional fairs "may require a project data book and/or a research paper." — [ISEF International Rules 2026-2027](https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/ISEF/2027/Rules/Book.pdf)
- 2027 ISEF forms are 1, 1A, 1B, 2A, 2B, 2C, 3, 4, 5A, 5B, 6A, 6B and 7, plus "Example Abstract with ISEF Categories" (file named 2027-22-Categories.pdf). — [ISEF Forms](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/forms/)

### Inferences
- SSEF uses the ISEF forms directly. Its own forms are the Abstract Form, the BSL-1/2 Checklists, the Verification of Informed Consent, the Mortality Report, and a Display & Safety Certification. Of these, only the Abstract Form, and probably the Display & Safety Certification, apply to a stock-market data project.
- For a statistics or econometrics project on public market data, these Florida-specific items matter most:
  - (1) a full citation for the data source in the research plan, such as a database or vendor name, access date and dataset description, not just a URL
  - (2) at least five non-rulebook sources
  - (3) AI use recorded in the bibliography, and under ISEF also in the Acknowledgements and on Form 2A
  - (4) no results or conclusions in the plan
  - (5) an addendum for any substantial change to the method
- The plan should be written in future tense. The 2026-27 supplement changed its sample phrasing to "will be performed," which signals that a plan is a pre-registration.

### Gaps
- I did not open the actual 72nd SSEF Abstract Form or its instructions in the Drive "Rules" or "For Students" folders, because the Drive embed did not expose file links. Its word limit and fields are unconfirmed. The ISEF limit is 250 words and one page.
- I found no explicit page limit for the research plan in ISEF or SSEF documents.
- I did not find an SSEF deadline for state paperwork in 2027. The Welcome page says only that paperwork is due "within 7 calendar days after the fair is held and no later than the established date in February."

## Q3. Category: Mathematics vs Behavioral & Social Sciences vs Systems Software for a statistics/econometrics stock-market project

### Takeaway
SSEF's category list has no economics or finance category. The closest fit is **Mathematics and Computational Sciences (MACO)**, whose subcategory "Probability and Statistics" covers the "study of statistical tools used to analyze and interpret data." Hillsborough sends students to the SSEF category definitions, so the same reasoning applies at the regional fair.

### Cited Findings
- Hillsborough's "Types of Investigations" page shows its categories only as an image, which was not readable. It says "For a detailed description of each category, Click here," linking to SSEF's categories page. — [HR Types of Investigations](https://sites.google.com/view/hillsborough-stem-fair/hrsef-types-of-investigations)
- SSEF lists 13 categories: ANIM, BEHA, BMED, CMBI, CHEM, EAEV, ENMS, ENEV, IMRS, MACO, MICR, PHYS and PLNT. — [SSEF Categories](https://ssefflorida.com/categories/)
- **MACO (Mathematics and Computational Sciences):** "The study of the measurement, properties, and relationships of quantities and sets…" Its subcategories include Algebra, Analysis, Combinatorics/Graph/Game Theory, Geometry and Topology, Number Theory, and **"Probability and Statistics: Mathematical study of random phenomena and the study of statistical tools used to analyze and interpret data."** It also covers "data-analytical and theoretical methods, mathematical modeling and computational simulation techniques to the study of biological, behavior, and social systems." — [SSEF Categories](https://ssefflorida.com/categories/)
- **BEHA (Behavioral & Social Sciences):** "the thought processes and behavior of humans and other animals… through observational and experimental methods." Its Sociology/Social Psychology subcategory says sociology "is concerned with all group activities-economic, social, political, and religious." — [SSEF Categories](https://ssefflorida.com/categories/)
- **IMRS (Intelligent Machines, Robotics and Systems Software):** "The study or development of software, information processes or methodologies to demonstrate, analyze, or control a process/solution." Subcategories include Machine Learning ("algorithms that can learn from data"), Algorithms and Databases. — [SSEF Categories](https://ssefflorida.com/categories/)
- SSEF's advice: many projects fit several categories. Ask "Who will be the most qualified to judge my project?" and "What is the emphasis of my project?" You "don't need to compete in the same category as in your regional or school competition." — [SSEF Welcome](https://ssefflorida.com/welcome/)
- Hillsborough's K-5 categories include "Math/Computer/Coding," which is not relevant for grades 6-12. — [2026-27 General Rules PDF (K-5)](https://files-backend.assets.thrillshare.com/documents/asset/uploaded_file/5557/Hcps/f5d327e4-b796-4c5a-8acc-02ceff2c109e/26.27-Hillsborough-Regional-Hybrid-STEM--Fair-GENERAL-RULES.updated-10.2026.pdf?disposition=inline)
- ISEF 2027 has 22 categories, per the filename "2027-22-Categories.pdf" on the ISEF Forms page. I did not read that file. — [ISEF Forms](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/forms/)

### Inferences
- If the project's main contribution is statistical method (testing, regression or robust regression, false-alarm rates, simulation), **MACO / Probability and Statistics** fits best at SSEF and should draw statistically trained judges.
- **BEHA** fits only if the emphasis is investor behavior or human decision-making studied through observation or experiment. Since BEHA is defined around "thought processes and behavior," a pure market-data econometrics project would be a weak match.
- **IMRS** fits only if the main contribution is software or a machine-learning or algorithmic system.
- At ISEF the category list is different (22 categories). If the project advances, check it separately using the ISEF category PDF.

### Gaps
- I could not read Hillsborough's own 6-12 category table (it is an image). It may use the SSEF 13 or a local variant.
- SSEF's categories page was last modified 2024-05-19 (page metadata), so it may not reflect any 2026-27 changes.
- I did not read ISEF's 2027 category definitions, including whether ISEF "Mathematics" covers statistics.

## Q4. Rules on writing the plan before experimentation, SRC approval timing, and revised plans

### Takeaway
All three levels (Hillsborough, SSEF, ISEF) require the research plan to be written and reviewed **before** experimentation. SRC pre-approval is mandatory only for humans, vertebrates, PHBAs and hazardous work, and Hillsborough recommends it for human studies. Changes go in an **addendum**, and SSEF requires one for any "substantial addition, deletion, or clarification."

### Cited Findings
- Hillsborough: "Students do NOT begin experimentation until required approvals are obtained and the Research Plan is reviewed." Forms 1, 1A with plan, 1B, 2A and 3 are "Required before experimentation." — [HCPS 6-12 Timeline](https://www.hillsboroughschools.org/page/stem-fair-grades-6-12-timeline/)
- Hillsborough SRC pre-approval is required for human subjects, PHBAs, vertebrate animals, and hazardous chemicals/activities/devices, and is requested through a Google Form. — [HR SRC Pre-Approval](https://sites.google.com/view/hillsborough-stem-fair/src-pre-approval-hrsef); [HR Paperwork Requirements](https://sites.google.com/view/hillsborough-stem-fair/hrsef-paperwork-requirements). The timeline calls it only "highly recommended" for human-subject studies. — [HCPS 6-12 Timeline](https://www.hillsboroughschools.org/page/stem-fair-grades-6-12-timeline/)
- After Jan 11, regional screening returns "Full Clearance, Edits Needed, and/or Potential Failure to Qualify," and corrections are due before Jan 25, 2027. — [HCPS 6-12 Timeline](https://www.hillsboroughschools.org/page/stem-fair-grades-6-12-timeline/)
- SSEF: "Teachers/Adult Sponsors are to critically review all Research Plans BEFORE experimentation or data collection begins." — [2025-26 SSEF Rules Supplement](https://www.stjohns.k12.fl.us/stem-fair/wp-content/uploads/sites/182/2025/07/2025-26-SSEF-Rules-Supplement.pdf). This line was not in the 2026-27 text I extracted, though the research-plan section still says "written prior to experimentation." — [2026-27 SSEF Rules Supplement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ImzJHKi8CdMCD2b_YQWpJaAhyEbQcH74/view)
- SSEF: an addendum is required for substantial changes, and adding humans, PHBAs, vertebrates or hazardous items may need re-approval. The plan "must not include data, conclusions, or unapproved procedures." — [2026-27 SSEF Rules Supplement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ImzJHKi8CdMCD2b_YQWpJaAhyEbQcH74/view)
- ISEF: the plan is written prior to experimentation. Changes made before competing at an affiliated fair "must be added as an addendum to the original plan." If there are no changes, no addendum is needed. — [ISEF 2027 Form 1A instructions](https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/ISEF/2027/Forms/1A-Student-Checklist-Research-Plan-Instructions.pdf)
- ISEF: all projects are reviewed by the affiliated fair SRC "after experimentation and before competition." Research covers at most 12 months, starting no earlier than January 1 of the year before the fair. — [ISEF International Rules 2026-2027](https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/ISEF/2027/Rules/Book.pdf); [2025-26 SSEF Rules Supplement](https://www.stjohns.k12.fl.us/stem-fair/wp-content/uploads/sites/182/2025/07/2025-26-SSEF-Rules-Supplement.pdf)

### Inferences
- A stock-market data project with no human participants needs no SRC pre-approval. It still needs Forms 1, 1A with the plan, 1B, 2A and 3 signed and dated (MM/DD/YY) **before** any analysis begins. The signature dates on 1A and 1B are the evidence that the plan came first.
- Any change to the analysis after the plan was signed should be written up in a dated addendum and filed with the Jan 11 upload. Examples: adding a robustness check such as robust regression, or changing the sample period. SSEF says flatly that projects without a complete plan "will not be approved" and cannot be resubmitted.

### Gaps
- No Hillsborough-specific rule on the format or deadline for revised plans or addenda was found beyond ISEF and SSEF.
- I found no statement on whether the regional fair treats pre-registered "expected outcomes" differently from a formal hypothesis.
